## Questions

1. What are 2-3 outcomes you delivered against your goals this review
   period? Bullet list, concise. Moments where you moved the needle, made
   a real impact on the business, or went above and beyond.
2. Cultural contribution: what's one way you contributed to our culture?
   (For example, exemplified a value, participated in a community, sought
   cross-functional feedback.)
3. What's one thing you'd do differently, and why? Intentional and
   concise: a moment you wished you had handled differently, or a
   challenge you faced, and the learning.
4. What are your top 2-3 goals for next quarter, and how will you know
   you've succeeded?

The employee does not rate themselves. The manager rates on the scale
below.

## Rating scale (manager only)

- Exceptional Impact: transformational impact at department or company
  level, well beyond role scope.
- Greater Impact: consistently beyond role expectations by expanding
  scope, higher-stakes work, or harder problems; a pattern.
- Effective Impact: meets role and level expectations; the standard for
  a fully performing employee.
- Partial Impact: partially meets expectations; results unreliable.
- Insufficient Impact: well below expectations.

Write outcomes so they read as Greater Impact evidence: scope widened,
stakes raised, teams crossed.
